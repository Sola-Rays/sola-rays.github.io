Client Requirements for the GSIS Loan

You must be

  • An active GSIS member with at least 3 years of government service
  • Up to date on GSIS premium contributions
  • Free of any defaulted GSIS loan (housing loans excepted)
  • Without a pending administrative or criminal case, and not on leave without pay
  • Meeting the required net take-home pay

How it works

  1. 1Get a free consultation and a solar quotation from SolaRays.
  2. 2Apply through the GSIS Touch mobile app and submit the quotation.
  3. 3Once approved, we install your system.
  4. 4Submit the installation documents (such as the contract or official receipt) to GSIS within the required period to keep your free insurance.

Good to know: Loan terms, eligibility and documents are set by GSIS and may change. Please confirm the latest requirements on the GSIS Touch app or the official GSIS page. Not sure if you qualify? Ask us and we'll guide you.

Understanding Solar Panel Placement in Mindanao (Davao Region)

Davao de Oro and the wider Davao Region sit only about 7° north of the equator, so the sun climbs almost overhead all year and drifts slightly north in summer and south in winter. For the best year-round yield, panels are usually pointed South (or close to it), with small adjustments toward Southeast or Southwest depending on roof slope and shading. A tilt of 10° to 15° works well: it catches strong midday sun and lets the rain wash the panels clean.

  • Orientation Face South (slight SE / SW adjustment)
  • Tilt angle 10° – 15°
  • Bonus Natural rainwater cleaning

How Solar Works (Simple 4-Step Flow)

  1. 1

    Solar panels absorb sunlight

    Photovoltaic cells convert daylight into DC electricity.

  2. 2

    Inverter converts DC to AC power

    Your inverter transforms DC into usable 220V AC power.

  3. 3

    Power flows into your home or business

    Appliances and lighting run directly on solar energy.

  4. 4

    Excess energy stored or credited back to the grid

    Surplus power charges batteries or earns you bill credits.
